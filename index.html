Treatments

Two treatments, done properly

We focus on the treatments that cost the most abroad, so your trip to Yerevan makes the biggest difference.

Veneers

Thin, custom-made shells bonded to the front of your teeth to correct shape, colour, gaps and chips.

  • Smile design agreed with you before any work begins
  • Material chosen for your teeth and your goals
  • Usually completed in one trip

Dental implants

A titanium root topped with a natural-looking crown, replacing missing teeth for the long term.

  • Scan-based treatment plan before you travel
  • From a single tooth to a full arch
  • Often two trips a few months apart, while the bone heals
Your price comes after your consultation

Every smile is different, so we don't publish fixed prices. Your doctor reviews your photos, talks with you on video, then sends your personal plan and exact price before you book any travel.
